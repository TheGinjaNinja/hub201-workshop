### Task 5a: `01-say-do-gap.md`

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the file**

Content:
1. H1 `# The Say-Do Gap`
2. Paragraph definition: people lie to be polite; what they say and what they do diverge.
3. H2 `## Why it matters for discovery` — behaviour is truth, opinions are noise.
4. H2 `## How to close the gap in interviews` — ask about past behaviour (last time they did X), ask for specific examples, observe rather than ask hypothetical.
5. H2 `## Classic failures` — example: "would you use this?" produces a yes-bias that dies on contact with real buying decisions.
6. Attribution: draws on Rob Fitzpatrick (The Mom Test), David Bland + Alex Osterwalder (Testing Business Ideas).

### Task 5b: `02-five-signals.md`

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the file**

Content:
1. H1 `# The Five Signals`
2. Paragraph framing: the signals the founder is listening for on every interview. Open questions are the tool; these are the purpose.
3. Five H2 sections, one per signal. For each: what the signal is, what question patterns elicit it, what a strong vs weak response looks like.
   - `## Recency and frequency` — when did you last do X; how often
   - `## Concrete behaviour` — give me a specific example; walk me through
   - `## Time and money spent` — what are you spending time on; where does your budget go
   - `## Problem-area match` — is what you're doing in our space or elsewhere (segmentation signal)
   - `## Awareness level` — do they know they have this problem, can we trigger awareness (market maturity)
4. H2 `## Applying the signals to script design` — each question in the interview script should be tagged with the signal(s) it elicits. A script is balanced when it covers all five.

### Task 5c: `03-open-question-rule.md`

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the file**

Content:
1. H1 `# The Open Question Rule`
2. H2 `## The rule` — use why, what, and how. Never do, would, could, or should. The purpose is to surface real experience, not test opinions on hypotheticals.
3. H2 `## Rewrite examples` — markdown table with Closed/Leading | Open Behavioural columns:
   - "Would you use this?" → "When did you last solve this, and how?"
   - "Do you think this is a problem?" → "Walk me through the last time this came up for you."
   - "Could you see yourself paying for this?" → "What did you spend on similar tools in the last 12 months?"
   - "Should we include feature X?" → "Show me how you handle this today."
4. H2 `## What the coach does with bad questions` — flags the pattern, shows the rewrite, explains why behavioural is stronger, never refuses.
