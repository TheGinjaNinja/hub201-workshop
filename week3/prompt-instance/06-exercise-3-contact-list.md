# Exercise 3: Contact List

## Purpose

Build a concrete, narrow list of 20-30 target interviewees with a channel-by-channel first-touch plan, so the team can start reaching out tomorrow.

## Inputs

- `hypotheses.md` (for ICP definition).
- `interview-plan.md` (so the team knows what they are inviting people to).

## Coaching flow

1. ICP narrowing drill. Take the segment from vague to specific, triggered, reachable. "Small businesses" is not an ICP. "Head of Security at a 200-500 person regulated fintech in DACH, CEE, or UK that has hired at least one SOC analyst in the last 12 months" is. The trigger (recent hire, recent incident, recent funding) is what makes them findable and currently interested.
2. Credibility check. Before the team sends any outreach, walk through the pre-outreach checklist from `shared/prompt-methodology/discovery/06-credibility.md`:
    - Is there a reasonable website? (Even a one-pager.)
    - Are founder LinkedIn profiles updated and professional?
    - Is there at least one piece of social proof? (Named advisor, press mention, event appearance, industry body association.)
    If any of these are missing, help the team draft this week's remediation actions. Never block outreach; start with lower-status targets while credibility improves.
3. Channel sourcing. Walk through the realistic channels for this ICP:
    - LinkedIn search queries (job title + company size + region + trigger).
    - Industry events in the region that match the segment.
    - Industry bodies or panels (NPL Markets lesson: being on a working group is a discovery superpower).
    - Advisory or Hub201 mentor network for warm intros.
    - Cold email and cold call with a personalised opener.
    - For B2C: value-first prototypes per the P.S. Bridal pattern.
4. First-touch templates. Write or refine templates for each channel the team plans to use.
5. Tracking. Build a simple contact sheet the team will actually use (name, role, company, why them, channel, first-touch plan, status).

## Output template

Generate as `/outputs/week3/contact-list.md`:

- **Header:** Startup name, date, exercise name.
- **Narrow ICP:** one sentence. Specific, triggered, reachable.
- **Credibility checklist:** three items with status (done / in progress / missing) and remediation actions where needed.
- **Target contact table:** 20-30 named targets. Columns: Name, Role, Company, Why them (fit + trigger), Channel, First-touch plan, Status.
- **Outreach templates:** personalised cold email, LinkedIn opener, cold call script, referral-ask template.
- **Channel mix:** recommended proportion across channels.
- **Evidence Assessment and Next Steps** per the shared output convention.

## Evidence check

Is the ICP specific enough that two different team members would independently identify the same person as a target? If not, the ICP is too wide and needs another pass of narrowing. Ask for a test case: "If you asked your co-founder to spend 30 minutes on LinkedIn finding targets right now, would they come back with people who match the ICP?"
