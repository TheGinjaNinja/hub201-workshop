# Hub201 Week 3: Setup

This guide gets your team started on Week 3. You have two options: a browser-based track for non-technical teams, or Claude Code for developers. Both lead to the same three deliverables, produced by an AI coach.

## Two tracks. Pick one.

Choose based on your team's comfort with command line tools. Both work equally well. The non-technical track is faster to start; the Claude Code track gives you more control and works offline.

## Non-technical track (easiest)

30 seconds from start to coaching.

1. Go to the launcher page: `week3/web/index.html` in your local repo, or `https://theginjaninja.github.io/hub201-workshop/week3/web/` online.
2. Click "Copy coach prompt" (the button copies the exact instructions to your clipboard).
3. Open [claude.ai](https://claude.ai) (free account works) or ChatGPT with GPT-4 or later, and start a new chat.
4. Paste the prompt and send.
5. Answer the coach's questions. It will take you through all three exercises.

## Technical track (Claude Code)

For teams comfortable with the terminal.

1. Install Claude Code per the repo-level setup guide in `../SETUP.md`.
2. `cd ~/Code/hub201-workshop` and open Claude Code against the week3 folder: `claude code week3/`
3. Claude Code reads `week3/CLAUDE.md` automatically and loads the coaching instructions.
4. Start the conversation. The coach takes it from there.

## What the coach will ask for

- Your startup one-liner and stage (idea, prototype, MVP, or on-market).
- Your branding: colours, logo, pitch deck if you have one.
- Which exercise to tackle first: Hypotheses, Interview Plan, or Contact List. The coach recommends the order but does not force it.

## What you'll leave with

- Three markdown output files in `/outputs/week3/`: `hypotheses.md`, `interview-plan.md`, `contact-list.md`
- One consolidated HTML presentation in your team's branding, ready to share with your Hub201 mentor.
- A ready-to-run interview plan for 5 interviews per week, aiming for 10 interviews by Week 4.

## If you get stuck

The coach never refuses to produce an output. If it feels like you don't have enough evidence, ask it to generate a "best-effort draft with the weak spots flagged" and it will. The coach will flag exactly where the evidence is thin.

If the coach cites a fact you can't verify, treat it as a suggestion, not truth. The coach is instructed never to invent, but always sense-check before taking anything into your interviews.
