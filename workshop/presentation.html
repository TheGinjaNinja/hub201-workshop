Today's exercise: test whether you are building on assumptions or evidence.

The Evidence Scale

Strong Someone paid, signed, or changed their behaviour
Moderate Someone committed time or reputation
Weak Someone said they liked it
Assumption You believe it but haven't tested it

250 LOIs = Weak. I treated it as Strong.

The Say-Do Gap

What people say and what people do are different things.

What they say

"I would definitely use that."

"Send me a proposal."

"We need this."

vs

What counts as evidence

They signed a contract.

They paid money.

They changed their behaviour.

Only behavioural evidence counts. Verbal enthusiasm is not evidence.

NPL MARKETS / WHAT 200 RESEARCH PARTICIPANTS REVEALED

A strong need with high complexity

1

Marketplace lacked a structured bidding process that protected participants, inhibiting trades

2

Extremely high legal costs for every transaction, creating big trades only

3

Brokers provided access to the same set of buyers, meaning pricing was poor

4

Data lacked a standard across the market, making assets difficult to price and trade

5

Pricing assets was difficult and took a very long time, limiting participation

These were not our assumptions. These were their words.

P.S. BRIDAL / WHAT DATA TOLD US

Key themes and ideas emerge

1

Ordering process is confusing, resulting in lower order volumes and less revenue

2

Brides want to try on a dress and reserve it for their wedding at the same time

3

Pricing is too high for try-ons, and too low for wedding reservations

High demand for alterations and international customers

KEY LESSONS

Cultivate an early-stage mindset

Let go of your ego

You are not your user. Any idea is just a starting point.

Stay focused on the problem

Find the right problem to solve, then solve it the right way.

Step away from the solution

Focus on solving the problem. No vitamins, only painkillers.

Focus on the customer

Regularly speak to customers and listen to their needs.

Launch and iterate

You should be embarrassed by your first product.

Sell before you build

Get customers to commit at every stage of the journey.

Do things that don't scale

Prioritise getting a few customers who love you over scalability.

Don't get trapped in the build

Stop building at some point and ask why.

Driven by data

Gut decisions are natural, but data drives stronger value.

KEY LESSONS

Think like your customer
