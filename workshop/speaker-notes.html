"250 organisations said they wanted it. Not one of them paid. I confused interest with evidence."
"Meanwhile, a competitor shipped faster, simpler, cheaper. We were liquidated."
Show evidence strength scale. Either Canva slide or just say it.

Evidence Strength Scale:

Strong Someone paid, signed, or changed their behaviour
Moderate Someone committed time or reputation
Weak Someone said they liked it
Assumption You believe it but haven't tested it
"250 LOIs? That's Weak evidence. I treated it as Strong. That mistake cost EUR 5 million and 30 people their jobs."
"How many of you have people saying they'd use your product? That's the weakest signal on the ladder."

"What changed? I was obsessed with proving that the customer had the problem before I built the solution."
Introduce the 3 pre-seed fits.

The 3 fits at pre-seed:

  1. Customer/Problem Fit: Does this customer have this problem badly enough to pay?
  2. Problem/Solution Fit: Does your solution actually solve it in a way they value?
  3. Revenue Model Fit: Can you charge for it in a way that builds a business?
"Most technical founders skip straight to number 2. They build the solution before proving the problem exists. That's what I did with Bondcube. NPL Markets worked because I did them in order."

16:47 The Framework "Here's what you're going to do today" (3 min)
Canva slides. Framework/fits slide.
"Most of you are in solution mode. You've got an idea, you're building it, and you're assuming customers will want it. That's Bondcube thinking."
"Today we're pulling you back to customer and problem mode."
"You're going to work with an AI startup coach that will walk you through 6 exercises: value proposition, competition matrix, differentiation statement, solution one-pager, personas, and a sales battlecard."
"The coach will ask tough questions. It will challenge vague answers. And it will rate every claim you make on that evidence scale: Strong, Moderate, Weak, or Assumption."
"Be honest with it. If you don't have evidence, say so. The exercises are designed to expose your gaps, not hide them."
"At the end, you'll have a professional HTML presentation that shows where your evidence is strong and where you need more discovery. That's your deliverable for our 1:1 sessions."
Transition: "Let's take some questions."

Ready responses for common questions:

"How is this different from a business model canvas?"
"A canvas is a map. These exercises are evidence tests. Every section forces you to rate whether your claims are backed by data or by hope."
"What if we're already on the market?"
"Great. Then you should have Strong evidence for most of your claims. Let's see if you do."
"We can't share our approach with competitors."
"You don't need to. The coach works with you individually. None of your outputs are shared. The exercises are about your customer positioning, not your codebase."
