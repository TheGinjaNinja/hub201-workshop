# ProcureSimple
**Stop overpaying suppliers. Save 10-15% with zero risk.**

## The Problem
SMEs with 50-500 employees spend 40-60% of revenue on procurement but manage it through spreadsheets and email. Operations managers negotiate alone with no visibility into total spend and no leverage. Rising input costs are squeezing margins and the board wants savings yesterday.

## Who This Is For
Operations Managers and Finance Directors at European manufacturing SMEs (50-500 employees) facing margin pressure from rising supplier costs.

## What Changes
**Before:** Your ops manager spends 2 days per month chasing invoices, negotiating alone, with no idea what the company actually spends per supplier.

**After:** A real-time spend dashboard, automated supplier benchmarking, and collective buying power that delivers 10-15% savings on targeted categories. The platform pays for itself from day one because you only pay a percentage of realised savings.

## How It Works
- **Connect and map.** Link your accounting system or upload invoices. The spend analytics engine categorises and maps your supplier spend in hours, not weeks.
- **Find the savings.** The platform benchmarks your rates against aggregated market data and identifies where you are overpaying.
- **Negotiate together.** ProcureSimple negotiates with suppliers on your behalf, combining your volume with other SMEs for better rates.
- **Pay only for results.** You pay a percentage of the savings actually delivered. No savings, no fee.

## Proof
- Currently in discovery with 6 manufacturing SMEs. Two have shared anonymised spend data for analysis.
- One supplier category (packaging) identified with an estimated 12% savings opportunity across 3 companies.
- No completed transactions yet. The 12% figure is based on market benchmarking, not a negotiated rate.

## Evidence Assessment
**Weak to Emerging.** No completed transactions and no realised savings yet. The proof section is honest about this, which is good. The 12% packaging savings estimate comes from market benchmarking rather than a negotiated rate. To strengthen: close one real negotiation and document the actual savings delivered. Even a single completed transaction with verified savings would shift this to "Emerging" with confidence.

## Coaching Notes
The founders initially led with "AI-powered spend analytics" as the headline. Through coaching, we reframed to lead with the savings outcome and the zero-risk pricing model. That is what ops managers actually care about. Nobody buys analytics. They buy margin improvement.

The original "How It Works" section had 7 bullets covering every feature. We cut it to 4 that tell a clear story from connection to results.

Biggest risk to flag: the collective buying power claim is aspirational with only 6 companies in discovery. If aggregated volume does not produce meaningfully better rates than individual negotiation, the core value proposition breaks. The next phase of discovery should specifically test whether suppliers will offer better pricing for combined SME volume, or whether this is just a theory.
