"Our collective procurement platform helps operations managers at European manufacturing SMEs with 50-500 employees who need to reduce supplier costs and get spend visibility by aggregating buying power across multiple companies and replacing manual spreadsheet-based processes with automated spend analytics, unlike traditional purchasing consultants who charge fixed retainers regardless of results and provide one-off recommendations rather than ongoing savings."

Exercise 3

Differentiation Statement

"For Operations Directors and Finance Managers at SMEs (50-500 employees) who waste time on manual procurement and have no visibility into what they spend with suppliers, ProcureSimple is a spend analytics and procurement platform that delivers measurable savings on a pay-for-performance basis. Unlike SpendMaster and ProcurePlus, we work from messy invoice data rather than clean ERP exports, price on a share of realised savings rather than per-seat licensing, and are built specifically for SMEs who cannot justify enterprise procurement tools."

Path: Adjacency

Customer segment + business model innovation

Taking procurement tools (existing category) to a new customer segment (SMEs) with a new business model (performance-based pricing) and a new capability (collective buying).

Defensibility

Building toward network effects

More customers means more buying power, which means better rates, which attracts more customers. This creates a flywheel. Theoretical at pre-seed stage. Has not been validated yet.

Evidence Checklist

  • Target customer clearly defined (SMEs, 50-500 employees, manufacturing)
  • Pain point validated through interviews (manual processes, no visibility)
  • Competitor analysis based on product research
  • Performance pricing preference stated in interviews (not tested with real money)
  • Collective buying power remains an untested thesis
  • No live product to validate "messy invoice data" claim

Summary

Evidence Assessment

ProcureSimple has moderate evidence for the problem and customer segment. The solution and business model are still largely assumption-based. Here is the honest picture.

Strongest Evidence

  • Cost pressure trigger: 6/6 interviews confirmed rising input costs
  • Spend visibility pain: 5/6 interviews confirmed no visibility into supplier spend
  • Manual process pain: 4/6 interviews confirmed spreadsheet-based procurement

Weakest Evidence

  • Collective buying power: Untested thesis. No buyer group has been formed.
  • Performance pricing acceptance: Stated preference only. Nobody has signed a contract.
  • Finance Director persona (Elena): Mostly inferred from Ops Manager conversations

What Needs Validating Next

1. Run a pilot

Get 3-5 SMEs to upload real invoice data. Test the platform end-to-end.

2. Test collective buying

Negotiate one collective supplier rate (packaging). See if the theory holds.

3. Validate revenue model

Will customers actually pay a share of savings? Test with real contracts.
