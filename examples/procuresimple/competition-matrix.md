# Competition Matrix: ProcureSimple

## 1. Competitive Alternatives

**Direct competitors:**
- SpendMaster (legacy procurement platform, now enterprise-focused after acquisition)
- ProcurePlus (procurement module within a major ERP suite)

**Adjacent products:**
- TradeLink (traditional procurement broker/consultant with no technology platform)

**Manual processes / workarounds:**
- Spreadsheets and email. The procurement manager tracks supplier contracts in Excel, negotiates by phone, and reconciles spend manually at month-end. Common in SMEs with 50-200 employees.
- Internal finance teams pulling spend reports from accounting software and negotiating individually with each supplier.

**Do nothing:**
- Accept fragmented supplier relationships and whatever pricing each department negotiates independently. Many SMEs in the 50-500 employee range have no centralised procurement function at all. Each department buys what it needs, when it needs it, from whoever they used last time.

**Notes on scoring honesty:**
- ProcureSimple scores a 2 on spend analytics because the prototype has basic spend categorisation and dashboards, but nothing close to SpendMaster's mature analytics engine with 10+ years of development behind it. This gap is real.
- ProcureSimple scores a 1 on ERP/accounting integration because the prototype currently connects to one accounting platform via a basic API. SpendMaster and ProcurePlus have deep, mature integrations across dozens of systems.
- ProcureSimple scores a 1 on contract management because this feature barely exists. It stores contracts as documents but has no lifecycle tracking, renewal alerts, or clause analysis.
- The collective buying power score of 3 reflects a working concept with early supplier agreements, not a proven network at scale. This could drop to 2 if supplier onboarding proves slower than expected.
- TradeLink scores a 5 on supplier negotiation because experienced brokers genuinely are excellent negotiators. That is their entire business. ProcureSimple's automated approach is unproven by comparison.

## 4. Qualifiers vs. Differentiators

**Qualifiers (table stakes):**
- Spend analytics / visibility: Every procurement tool needs some form of spend tracking. ProcureSimple needs credible dashboards to be taken seriously, but analytics alone will not win deals against SpendMaster.
- Reporting / dashboards: Customers expect to see where their money goes. This is a baseline expectation, not a competitive advantage.
- Contract management: Basic contract storage and tracking is expected. ProcureSimple's current gap here (scored 1) is a liability that needs closing to reach minimum viability.

**Differentiators (ProcureSimple's edge):**
- Collective buying power: No direct competitor offers this for SMEs. SpendMaster does not aggregate demand across customers. ProcurePlus does not either. TradeLink does it manually for individual clients but cannot pool buying power across its client base systematically. This is the clearest structural differentiator.
- Pricing for SMEs: Performance-based pricing (percentage of savings) versus per-seat subscriptions fundamentally changes the risk profile for an SME buyer. They pay only when they save. SpendMaster and ProcurePlus charge regardless of outcomes.
- Ease of deployment and onboarding speed: SMEs will not tolerate 3-6 month implementations. ProcureSimple's lightweight onboarding is a genuine advantage over SpendMaster and ProcurePlus for the target segment.

**Irrelevant (for now):**
- Deep ERP integration: SMEs in the 50-500 range often run basic accounting software, not full ERP suites. Building enterprise-grade ERP connectors is not a priority for the beachhead. Basic accounting software integration (Xero, QuickBooks, Sage) matters more than SAP or Oracle connectors.
- Advanced RFQ / tendering automation: Most SMEs in this range do not run formal tender processes. They get three quotes by email and pick one. Sophisticated tendering workflows are over-engineering for the beachhead.

**Aspirations:**
- Spend analytics improving to a 4 or 5: As ProcureSimple aggregates more supplier data across its customer base, the analytics should become genuinely distinctive. Benchmarking a customer's spend against anonymised peers would be a powerful feature that no current competitor offers at the SME level. This requires significant customer volume first.
- Supplier negotiation powered by data: Today TradeLink wins on negotiation through human expertise. If ProcureSimple can combine collective buying power with spend data to negotiate algorithmically, this moves from aspiration to differentiator. Unproven at this stage.

## 5. Positioning Opportunity Statement

**Path: Adjacency**

ProcureSimple is positioned as an adjacent player to enterprise procurement platforms. The adjacency is defined by two dimensions: customer segment (SMEs with 50-500 employees, below the typical enterprise threshold of SpendMaster and ProcurePlus) and business model (performance-based percentage of savings, not per-seat SaaS subscription).

SpendMaster could theoretically serve this market, but it would require simplifying a complex product, cutting prices by 80%+, redesigning onboarding for teams without dedicated procurement staff, and accepting a fundamentally different revenue model. Since their acquisition, they have moved in the opposite direction. ProcurePlus would need to decouple procurement from the parent ERP, build a standalone product, and create SME-friendly pricing. Their entire business model depends on ERP lock-in. TradeLink serves the right customers but cannot scale beyond the capacity of individual consultants and has no technology platform to offer analytics or automation.

The gap is structural, not a feature gap. Serving SMEs with performance-based procurement requires different product design, different pricing architecture, and different go-to-market. Incumbents would need to cannibalise their existing models to compete.

| Performance-based pricing is preferred over per-seat | 3 interviewees said they preferred paying based on savings. No pricing experiments conducted. No willingness-to-pay data. | Weak |
| ProcurePlus is overkill for SMEs | Desk research on implementation requirements and pricing. 1 interviewee had evaluated ProcurePlus and rejected it as too complex. | Weak |
| TradeLink-style brokers cannot scale | Observation from 2 interviews with companies that use procurement consultants. Not formally validated. | Assumption |

**Overall assessment: Moderate with Assumption pockets.** The problem signal is consistent across 6 interviews: SMEs are overpaying and have no visibility. The solution claims (collective buying power, performance pricing) are logical but unvalidated by any competitive deal or transaction. The biggest gap is that ProcureSimple has not yet won or lost a single competitive deal. Until the team sees how a real SME evaluates ProcureSimple against the alternatives listed above, the positioning is theory, not evidence.

## 7. Next Steps

1. Win (or lose) a competitive deal. The fastest way to validate this positioning is to put ProcureSimple in front of 3-5 SMEs alongside the alternatives and see what happens. Losing is as informative as winning. Document why they chose or rejected each option.
2. Run a pricing experiment. Show 5 SME procurement leads two models: GBP 30/user/year flat rate versus 10% of documented savings. Measure preference and probe the reasons. "We think performance pricing is better" is an assumption until tested.
3. Build the basic accounting integrations (Xero, QuickBooks, Sage) to close the table stakes gap. Without connecting to the systems SMEs actually use, onboarding friction will kill conversion regardless of the value proposition.
4. Validate collective buying power with at least one supplier category. Pick the most common indirect spend category from discovery interviews (e.g. office supplies, IT hardware, telecoms) and negotiate a group rate with one supplier. Prove the model works at small scale before claiming it as a differentiator.
5. Talk to 2-3 former SpendMaster SME customers. Understand why they left, what they switched to, and what they wish existed. These are the most valuable prospects for ProcureSimple and the most informative source of competitive intelligence.
6. Close the contract management gap. A score of 1 on a qualifier capability is a disqualification risk. Basic contract storage with renewal reminders and expiry alerts would move this to a 3 and remove a reason for prospects to reject ProcureSimple early in evaluation.
