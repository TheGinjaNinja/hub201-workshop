3 Differentiation Statement

How we stand apart

MVP Differentiation Statement

For CISOs and IT Security Managers at mid-market Serbian banks (200-1,000 employees) who need to implement NIS2-compliant security awareness programmes, ShieldByte is an adaptive phishing simulation platform that reduces phishing susceptibility with measurable, auditable results. Unlike KnowBe4, we deliver Serbian-language simulations with locally relevant phishing scenarios, NIS2-mapped compliance reporting, and pricing built for mid-market banks rather than global enterprise.

Path to Differentiation

Adjacency

Geography + customer segment. Taking an established category (phishing simulation) and adapting it for an underserved region and market tier.

Defensibility

Building toward proprietary data (regional phishing patterns, click-rate benchmarks across Balkan banks) and regulatory advantage (deep NIS2 mapping that global players won't prioritise for this market).
